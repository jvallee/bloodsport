# Bloodsport v5 (frozen)

A playable snapshot of the game as of tag `v5.0` (2026-10-28), served at https://jvallee.github.io/bloodsport/v5/.
New since v4:
- Vampire and Basa kinds, and the Stables
- Retiarius, Ash-Hungry and Blood-Bearer classes
- Axe and Dagger, and weapon matchups
- five new Apothecary items, Imperial gear, new decor, perks, Boss arena rules and events
- more deaths, with the death risk shown in Mortal Danger

Refreshed on request (2026-10-28) with a v5 update: Power breakdown on hover and live Power in fights, a clearer round-winner reveal, a bigger Armourer stock, roomier Quarters and kinder tournament crowds. No new tag, at the user's request.
Refreshed again (2026-10-28) with signature moves, thumbs up or down, an economy pass and the playtest #6 fixes. No new tag, on request.
Refreshed again (2026-10-28) with the designer's playtest #7 notes: tournament bracket on the fight screen, auto-play fixes, foldable Market with a Stables section, Unequip all gear, Championships that cascade to lower arenas (with tougher later arenas), and weaker prisoners. No new tag, on request.
Patched on request (2026-10-28): long fighter names no longer hide behind the Power/Speed badges (style.css only). No new tag.

Refreshed on request (2026-11-09) to **5.4.3**, the last of the 5.x line, before version 6: quests, rival houses, the Emperor's summons, the Liaison line-up and everything since.
