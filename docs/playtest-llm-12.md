# Playtest LLM-12 — seed 12012 (version 6.0.0-alpha.4, a season)

## 1. Summary

- **Seed 12012, a season (the default career). About 430 commands sent, 405 kept in the replay file** (I removed refused and read-only ones). **18 months played**, Martius wk 1, Y1 to Sextilis wk 1, Y2. The career had not ended: I stopped at the command budget, three weeks before my first Imperial Boss. So I never saw the Grand Tournament or the end-of-career Legacy XP screen.
- **Result so far: three of the four arenas conquered.**
  - **Dirt Pits:** Boss (Verus, Power 7) beaten at the first try, Aprilis Y1, by Fabius. House level 3.
  - **Bronze took 6 months and three Boss tries**, all against Decima of the Red Hand (Power 16 natural plus a Gladius):
    - Iunius Y1: Fabius, 16 + Berserker Mushroom + War Paint + Hawk Strike, against 19. Lost 0–2.
    - Sextilis Y1: Crixus, 20 against 20 with four items. Lost 0–2 on rolls of 96.7 and 59.5 (needing 48 and 53).
    - October Y1: Crixus, 20 against 20 (Decima now had +1 from a feud I'd caused). Won 2–0.
  - **Silver took 8 months and three Boss tries**, all with Glabra (Silver Wolves) in the Final:
    - December Y1: Crixus won the Quarter-final and the Semi-final, then lost the Final 1–2 in Round 3 to Cast the Net. −20 Favour, down to 25 (DANGER).
    - Aprilis Y2: Fabius won the Quarter-final, then won the Semi-final with **All In** at the Death Gate (5 injuries). I withdrew him from the Final (21% to win, 39% to die).
    - Iunius Y2: Crixus won all three, the Final 2–0 with four items and Haymaker.
  - **Imperial T1** (Quintilis Y2): Opimia (Power 31) won the Quarter-final, then lost the Semi-final 1–2 in Round 3.
  - **At stop:** Gold 87, Prestige 194 (House level 5), Favour 52. #2 of 4 houses in Glory (86, against the Red Hand's 145). The Imperial Boss is Krell, Power 36 (33 natural). My best fighter is 33 and at every cap. The Oracle gives my best chance as 7%.
- **Roster at stop** (all four started at Natural Power 6 or 7):
  - **Opimia:** Orc Ash-Hungry, Lv 5, Power 33 (21 training, 3 perks, 2 Titles, Gladius). My first recruit, and the strongest at the end.
  - **Fabius:** Orc Ornitharius, Lv 5, Power 30. Champion of the Dirt Pits. He killed Thraxa, the Red Hand's champion.
  - **Crixus:** Orc Cestus, Lv 6 (the maximum), Power 26. Champion of the Dirt Pits, Bronze and Silver. Renown 45, Discontented for most of Y2.
  - **Aemilius:** Basa Blood-Bearer, Lv 3, Power 15. A free fighter from the Fortune draft. Champion of the Dirt Pits.
- **Deaths:** none of mine. Seven opponents died: Thraxa, Novius, Rusta, Valens, Flamma and Pollux in the arena, and others by my thumbs. Pollux was the Elf in my very first Market, and Carpophorus, from the second, turned up as an Imperial regular. Lovely touch.
- **Relics (6/6 by November Y1):** Lucky Knucklebone, Trophy Wall, Mars's Whetstone, Drillmaster's Rod, Bronze Aegis, Bribed Referee.
- **Shape of the run:**
  - A fast Dirt Pits (two months).
  - A Gold famine for most of Y1: Gold was 0–8 in 20 of the 32 weeks from Aprilis to October.
  - Two Bronze walls, where my fighters sat at the training cap.
  - A rich Silver: purses of 27–47 and bracket wins of 9 each.
  - A Favour scare: 25 after the December loss.
  - Then a third wall at Imperial: the Boss's natural Power equals my best fighter's total.

## 2. FUN rubric

### Moments I wanted to keep playing ("one more month")

- **Maius Y1, Bronze T1: Fabius 13 against Dacia 14, shown at 30%.**
  - I pressed twice, and the crowd hit 40 and then 55, which gave +1 Power in Round 2.
  - I lost Round 2 but won Round 3 on a Guard read. +25 Gold when I had 3.
  - It was the best fight of the run, and the only one where the crowd meter mattered.
- **September Y1: Fabius killed Thraxa, the Red Hand's named champion, in a Bronze T1.**
  - "The Lanista of The Red Hand leaves before the crowd does" is a great line.
  - The consequence was even better: the Red Hand swore a feud, so their Boss Decima fought me at +1 the next month. My own kill made my wall higher. That is a real story.
- **October Y1: Crixus beat Decima at the third try**, 2–0 on a Guard read and then a Haymaker. This was the biggest relief of the run, and I instantly wanted to see Silver.
- **December Y1, the Silver Boss Final.**
  - Crixus 1–1 with Glabra going into Round 3. She unleashed Cast the Net (with no warning in the text port, see bug 2), and I lost on a 43% roll.
  - It was frustrating, but it was the closest thing to "one more go" in the whole run.
- **Aprilis Y2: Fabius at the Death Gate in the Silver Boss Semi-final.**
  - He had five injuries. The screen gave about 27% death fighting on, and about 19% going All In.
  - All In won the round at 80% and killed Valens.
  - Then the Final screen said 21% to win and 39% to die, and I withdrew him. Leaving a Final because the Death Gate was too wide open felt like a real Lanista's choice.
- **Iunius Y2: the Silver Boss won.**
  - I used Mushroom, War Paint, Iron Tonic, Throwing Net and Haymaker on two correct reads, and won 2–0.
  - The Emperor's "kill" demand was met by a death in the Quarter-final. That opponent was Pollux, the Elf I'd passed over in Martius Y1.

### Dead stretches

- **Sextilis to September Y1 (two months).** All three main fighters were at the Trainer level 3 cap (16 Power) and the Bronze Boss was 18–19. Gold was 1–9, so I couldn't buy the Trainer upgrade (26) or items. Every Champion was jeered in the lower arenas. The weeks were just `end-week`, and an event when I was lucky.
- **Winter Y1 (four weeks).** Nothing to do. I bought two decor items for Crixus and ended weeks.
- **Maius Y2.** Gold 4, two Discontented fighters, and nothing to train. The only decision of the month was the Fortune card.
- **The Imperial wall.** I stopped there, but the numbers say it would drag: the Boss is 36 and my fighters are at the Trainer level 5 cap (21 points). Market fighters at this tier are Power 9–13. There is no lever left except items and dice.
- **Why the walls drag.**
  - Every Boss so far had about 2 more natural Power than my fighters' *maximum* at the current Trainer level.
  - So each arena was: reach the cap, lose to the Boss, save for the next Trainer level (26, then 40 Gold), retrain at 4–5 Gold per fighter-week, then win.
  - It's a Gold timer, not a puzzle. The fights between are good, but the month-to-month rhythm is "wait".

### Decisions that felt meaningful

- **Who fights the Boss.** Scouting (+1 Power for a fighter who fought here last month) made me rest my best fighter in Silver T1 and send someone else, so the Boss saw a fresh face. That's good.
- **Thumbs up or down, when it changes something.** A kill could start a feud (it did, with the Red Hand) and a spare could create a debt (−1 Power against me for 3 months). I did weigh these.
- **Spending items** in Round 1 or saving them for the Final of a bracket.
- **Withdrawing Fabius from the Silver Final.**
- **Feint or Press when the tell and the humour disagree.** With a Choleric "loves to Press" and a tell of "Feint", Press was the hedge (it beats Feint and ties with Press). Working that out felt clever every time.

### Decisions that felt fake or obvious

- **Fortune, once the relic slots filled (November Y1).**
  - Before that, a relic was always the pick.
  - After it, the draft was mostly Senator's Nod, a Purse of Coin, a piece of gear or Trader's Crate. I took Senator's Nod three times because my Favour was low, which felt like paying a bill, not drafting.
  - I never spun (3 Gold when I had 4).
  - I never took the Fates' Wager (offered four times): losing it cost 6–9 Gold when I had almost none.
- **Perks.** Mighty whenever it was offered; otherwise whatever helped the next Boss.
- **Lower arenas once everyone was a Champion.** From Sextilis Y1 every fighter was Champion of the Dirt Pits, and from December Y1 two were Champions of Bronze. So the lower arenas were "−3 Favour, −10 Morale" for everyone except a rookie. I had no rookie and no room to buy one (quarters full until Iunius Y2).
- **The poaching warnings** ("The Red Hand has its eye on Draven… buy them this week") came three times, always with my quarters full. I couldn't act on any.
- **The Emperor's summons (September Y1).** Refusing costs 40 Favour, so I had to accept. Then "yield" was obviously right (the opponent was Power 32 against my 15). Opimia survived, and I got +5 Favour.

### The fights: crowd, tells and All In

- **Crowd meter.**
  - It reached the 35 mark once in about 30 fights (Fabius v Dacia above). The highest it reached was 55, and in most fights it sat at 0–15.
  - The reason: a correct read is a Guard or a Feint two times in three, and Guard drains the crowd. So the crowd rewards the wrong play.
  - It also resets every match, so in a three-match bracket it never builds.
  - The text port doesn't show the crowd's Gold and Renown payout at the end (bug 6), so I didn't know it paid at all.
  - Verdict: a nice gauge with almost no effect on how I played.
- **Tells.**
  - Over the run they were right 53 times in 75: 14 of 25 at Oracle level 1 (70%), and 39 of 50 at level 2 (85%).
  - Early on they felt like lies: 4 of the first 5 were wrong, and at level 1 I trusted them half the time.
  - I checked the code with a script outside the repo (thousands of matches from my own career's states): 69% and 84%, as designed. So it was bad luck, not a bug. But a player only sees 25 tells at level 1, and those can easily feel like a coin flip.
  - A 70% tell against a 70% humour lean really is close to 50/50, so Oracle level 1 rarely changes the right pick.
  - At level 2 the tells drove my choices and mostly paid off. "9 times in 10" overstates 85%, though (bug 4).
- **Reading right and losing anyway** was the biggest source of frustration:
  - November Y1: Crixus, Delta +4 and a correct Feint read, lost 0–2.
  - Maius Y2: Opimia, 83% to win the whole tournament, lost the Semi-final.
  - Quintilis Y2: Opimia, +2 on a correct read, lost Round 3.
  - The counter bonus (+10%) is the same size as the opponent's "First Strike +10%", which they got in nearly every round against my Speed 3 Orcs. So reading the opponent often just cancels Speed.
- **All In.**
  - It came up three times:
    - Martius Y1, my very first fight: 16% death against 13% steady. I won.
    - Aprilis Y2, the Silver Semi-final: 19% against 27%. I won and killed Valens.
    - The Silver Final screen: 39% against 28%. I withdrew.
  - The tension is real, especially when the death numbers are side by side.
  - But in the Semi-final, All In was both safer *and* stronger. It wasn't a gamble, it was the obvious pick.
  - The narration printed "Fabius undefined; Valens Feints." at the most dramatic moment of the run (bug 1).
- **Did I read the opponent?** Yes, every round. The humour-plus-tell puzzle is the best thing in the fights.

### Would I start another career straight away?

- **Probably yes, but not for the reasons the designer is aiming at.** I'd do it for the fights, the Boss moments and the little stories (the feud, Pollux coming back, the champion's death).
- Two things would stop me before the third arena:
  - The long Gold-timer walls.
  - The feeling that a correct read is worth less than a die roll.
- I'd also like to know what Legacy XP gives me, and I never got to see it. Nothing during the run told me what I was earning toward.
- If each wall offered two or three ways over it (gear, a better recruit, a relic, a Senate policy) instead of one Trainer upgrade, I'd start the next one straight away.

## 3. Where I was confused

- **The Fortune draft was hidden behind events.**
  - In Aprilis wk 1, Y1 the month began with an event and no Fortune cards on screen. I only found out the draft was waiting by trying `fortune 0` blind, and it took a Fates' Wager I hadn't seen (in a test, not in the kept replay).
  - The cards only appear once the event is decided (`scripts/play-text.js:308`, `!run.event`). A one-line "Fortune's wheel is waiting (decide the event first)" would help.
- **"Strong favourite" odds felt wildly different for similar fighters.** In Martius Y2 Crixus (23) was "86% for the whole Silver tournament", while Fabius (22) was 35%. It's probably the Challenger, Scouted and trait differences, but nothing on the card says why.
- **"Floralia: wins pay +50% Gold"** — the listed purse already includes the bonus, so I couldn't tell whether it applied. Something like "+24 Gold (16, +50% Floralia)" would be clearer.
- **The Signature line said it was available when it wasn't** (Fabius was Maimed; bug 3).
- **Advice after a Round-3 loss** said "Crixus was the stronger fighter (Delta +2)". The decider was 24 against 24; the average included Round 1, which my items had pumped up (`src/narrate.js:159`).
- **Repeatable perks.** Swift was offered again after Crixus had taken it, without saying it stacks. It's `repeatable`, but the card doesn't say so (`src/run.js:2238`).
- **Lower-arena rules text** reprints in full under every arena on every screen, which buries the odds lines.

## 4. Bugs

1. **All In prints "undefined" in the round narration.**
   - Martius Y1 ("Opimia undefined; Vaelin Guards.") and Aprilis Y2 ("Fabius undefined; Valens Feints.").
   - `VERB` in `src/narrate.js:25` has steady, press, guard and feint but no `allIn`, and line 30 indexes it.
   - The GUI uses the same `narrateStory` and `narrateRound` (`src/ui/fightView.js:49`), so it's broken there too.
   - Fix: add `allIn: 'goes All In'`.
2. **The text port never warns that the opponent will unleash their signature next round.** `opponentUnleashesNext` (`src/fight.js:549`) is used only in `src/ui/fightView.js:232`. In December Y1 Glabra's Cast the Net came in the deciding Round 3 with no warning, though the rule says it's certain.
3. **The Signature line offers a disabled move.**
   - Aprilis Y2: Fabius was Maimed. The screen said "Signature move (`signature`, once per fight): Hawk Strike", and `signature` then refused: "needs the class ability, and it's disabled this match".
   - `scripts/play-text.js:447` doesn't check `signatureBlocker`, which the GUI imports.
4. **The tell accuracy label overstates Oracle level 2.** `Math.round(0.85 * 10)` shows "right about 9 times in 10" (`scripts/play-text.js:442`, `src/ui/fightView.js:231`). Better: "about 85% of the time", or "8 or 9 times in 10".
5. **Grammar:** "their house already owes you a life (1 months left)" (`scripts/play-text.js:254`).
6. **The text port hides the crowd's payout.** `payHype` (`src/run.js:2729`) adds Renown and Gold to the result, and the GUI shows a "Crowd hype" row (`src/ui/fightView.js:126`). The text port's Result line just folds it into the totals.
7. **Advice uses the average Delta, inflated by one-round items** (`src/narrate.js:155–159`). Judging by the average round chance first (the line after) would be more honest.
8. **Text-port artefact (not a game bug):** with `--last`, Codex discovery only happens for rendered commands. So `codex building:oracle` as the first rendered command said "???" for an entry the list showed as discovered (`scripts/play-text.js:407`), and the "New in the Codex" list reprints in full on the first rendered command.

### Fixed since LLM-11 (confirmed)

- **Skipped steward tasks are now acknowledged and paid:** "already done: Have a fighter who holds two Titles; Reach House level 3; Upgrade the Trainer Room to level 3. +3 Gold."
- **The rival champion showed up.** Thraxa (Red Hand) was named in Maius Y1 and met in September Y1, four months later, in a Bronze T1 (not only as a Final). Bragga, their next champion, beat Opimia in a Silver Semi-final in Maius Y2.
- **The Emperor's met and missed lines** now print in the text port ("I asked you for one thing, Lanista. One.").
- **`end-week` with a pending perk is now refused**, rather than appearing to work.

## 5. Top 10 suggestions (most important first)

1. **Give each wall several ways over it, not one Gold timer.**
   - My Bosses kept sitting about 2 Power above my fighters' cap at the current Trainer level (Bronze: 18–19 against 16; Imperial: 36 against 33).
   - Options:
     - Make a Boss beatable at the cap plus items (cap +1 instead of +2–3).
     - Let the Fortune draft, an Armourer upgrade or a Senate policy offer a real +2 (a "Boss-breaker" card).
     - Sell seasoned Market fighters whose natural Power keeps pace with the tier, so a Natural-6 roster isn't a permanent handicap at Imperial.
2. **Make a correct read worth more than the opponent's Speed.** Raise the counter bonus to +15%, or let a correct read cancel the opponent's First Strike bonus. Then reading the opponent decides fights, which is the fun part. Losing 0–2 at Delta +4 on correct reads happened three times.
3. **Make the crowd reward the right plays.** Let a correct counter of any kind ("the crowd sees the read") and wounds fill it, not only Press. Carry part of it across a bracket's matches. Show its payout in the text port. As it stands, the crowd mattered once in 30 fights.
4. **Combine tell and humour into one honest forecast**, for example "Likely Press (about 65%)". Or raise Oracle level 1 to 80%. A 70% tell against a 70% humour is a coin flip, and in a short career it feels like the Oracle lies.
5. **Make All In a gamble with a unique payoff, and fix its narration (bug 1).** At five injuries it was safer than fighting on, so it wasn't a choice. Give it its own reward (fill the crowd, +Renown, a Bloodied Victory bonus) and its own cost (always a scar). Show the steady, Guard and All In death risks side by side.
6. **Keep the Fortune draft interesting after the relic slots fill.** Offer relic upgrades or swaps ("trade the Trophy Wall for…"), wagers scaled to your purse, and boons that change the month (a free Trainer week, a rookie with high natural Power, a Boss's arena rule revealed or changed). Three Senator's Nods in a row is a tax, not a draft.
7. **Give spare Champions something to do.** A "title defence" bout in the lower arena they hold (small purse, no jeer), or a sparring purse, so the two or three fighters not in the top arena aren't idle or punished for months.
8. **Ease the Y1 Gold famine.** Gold was 0–8 in 20 of the 32 weeks from Aprilis to October Y1. Pay a small win bonus in lower arenas even to Champions, or make the first Trainer upgrades cheaper. Every famine week was an `end-week` week.
9. **Fame upkeep for stars.** Crixus at Renown 45 expected −60 comfort and stayed Discontented with a Bathhouse, a Trophy Rack and Quarters level 3. Let Titles or a Champion's banner add comfort, or make the fame expectation level off. Otherwise the best fighter is always the unhappiest.
10. **Text-port polish:**
    - Show the Fortune cards (or "waiting") while an event is open.
    - Warn about the opponent's signature (bug 2).
    - Grey out a disabled signature (bug 3).
    - Print the lower-arena rules once rather than under every arena.
    - Show what Legacy XP the career is earning, during the run as well as at the end.
